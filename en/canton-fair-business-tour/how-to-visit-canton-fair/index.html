How to Visit the Canton Fair

To visit the Canton Fair, you need to choose the right phase in advance, prepare documents, apply for a China visa, complete visitor registration and plan your travel route to Guangzhou.

Main Steps for the Trip

A trip to the Canton Fair is not ordinary tourism. It is important to understand your goal in advance, choose the right phase and prepare the documents properly so the trip goes smoothly.

1

Choose the Exhibition Phase

First, you need to decide which product categories you need: electronics, home goods, clothing, textiles or other areas.

2

Prepare Documents

For the trip, you will need a passport, visa information, booking details, insurance and other data depending on the route.

3

Apply for a China Visa

We help with the visa application form, document preparation and support throughout the visa process.

4

Complete Registration

To visit the exhibition, visitor registration is required, along with the necessary details for receiving an attendee badge.

5

Plan the Route

It is important to choose flights, a hotel and convenient logistics to the exhibition complex in Guangzhou in advance.

6

Prepare for Negotiations

Before the trip, it is better to prepare a list of products, suppliers, questions and terms to discuss on-site.

We can help you complete registration, choose the exhibition phase, apply for a visa and organize your trip to Guangzhou.

What to Prepare in Advance

The better you prepare before the trip, the more useful your visit to the exhibition will be. This is especially important if your goal is to find suppliers, compare terms and negotiate purchases.

Documents and Travel

  • Passport
  • China visa
  • Medical insurance
  • Flight tickets and hotel booking
  • Contacts and accommodation address

For Working at the Exhibition

  • List of products you are looking for
  • Purchase budget
  • Questions for suppliers
  • Translator or communication support
  • Phone with WeChat and internet access
